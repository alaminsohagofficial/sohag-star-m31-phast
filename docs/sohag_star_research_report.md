# SOHAG STAR: A Photometric and Astrometric Investigation of Distinctive Stellar Sources in M31 (PHAST)

**Proponent:** Md. Al Amin Sohag  
**Ecosystem:** Salsabilah Ecosystem (Powered by Gemini)  
**Target Survey:** Hubble Space Telescope — Panchromatic Hubble Andromeda Southern Treasury (PHAST; Chen et al. 2025, *ApJ*, 979, 35; Bricks `B24`–`B36`)  

## 1. Overview & Scientific Objective
This report outlines a reproducible, archive-driven investigation into the southern star-forming disk of the Andromeda Galaxy (M31) using HST PHAST multi-band photometry (`F275W`, `F336W`, `F475W`, and `F814W`). The goal is to identify a distinct, astrometrically and photometrically verified stellar source that may be informally commemorated as **SOHAG STAR** once strict quality and cross-verification criteria are satisfied.

## 2. Historical Benchmark (M31-V1)
Cepheid variable **M31-V1**, identified by Edwin Hubble in 1923, established that M31 lies beyond the Milky Way. Within this project, M31-V1 serves strictly as a historical benchmark for resolved extragalactic stellar astronomy and is never treated as a candidate or proxy for a PHAST source.

## 3. Selection & Verification Gates
Every candidate source must pass six gates before receiving an informal commemorative label:
1. **Catalog Traceability:** Preserve exact PHAST source identifier, brick (`B24`–`B36`), and archive provenance.
2. **Astrometric Precision:** Record archive Right Ascension and Declination (J2000) directly from catalog tables.
3. **Photometric & Quality Filtering:** Require valid multi-band magnitudes, reliable signal-to-noise ratios, and clean crowding/sharpness quality flags.
4. **Astrophysical Distinctiveness:** Target clear regimes such as UV-bright hot stars or luminous red stars with well-defined color and brightness properties.
5. **Variability Discipline:** Treat PHAST data as multi-filter/dithered survey photometry rather than a standalone periodic light curve; claim variability only when supported by adequate time-series or external catalog evidence.
6. **Independent Cross-Verification:** Inspect cutouts and cross-check external catalogs/literature prior to final documentation.

## 4. Archival & Publication Roadmap
- **GitHub (`alaminsohagofficial/sohag-star-m31-phast`):** Version-controlled SQL/ADQL queries, Python filtering utilities, and documentation.
- **Zenodo:** Deposit versioned releases and data products to obtain a persistent DOI.
- **RNAAS / arXiv (`astro-ph`):** Once a verified candidate source is confirmed from live PHAST data, prepare a concise research note or preprint documenting the verified catalog record and photometry.
